## Life

Amsterdam is home now!! After months on the move, with a long stop in Belgrade, Serbia, I've finally unpacked for good. I've even worked out how to [ride the trams for less](/reflections/gvb-korting/).

Moving through life with more intention. At the moment that means focusing on health: sleep hygiene and training.

There is freedom in having a thought out structure to life. A routine is something I've been iterating on for years, and this latest iteration is the culmination of all my efforts. I'm really happy with it.

Up with the sun, creatine and amino water, no phone for the first hour. Journal and walk before the office 2-3 days a week with consistent hours, gym on the days I'm home. No screens from 9pm, then reading before bed. Fasting until lunch, eating only between 12pm and 6pm. I use pomodoro timers to gamify focus time across my days.
