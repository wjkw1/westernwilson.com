Public transport is super accessible in Amsterdam. Since moving here I've been using it a lot: trams into town, the metro to the office, buses for everything in between. It all adds up though, so I was hoping there was some sort of subscription or concession card that could make it slightly cheaper for me overall.

And I found something!! [GVB Korting](https://www.gvb.nl/gvbkorting)

## What GVB Korting is

GVB Korting (called GVB Flex until October 2026) is a monthly add-on for your bank card or OV-pas. You pay a small fixed fee, and every GVB trip after that is cheaper.

| Discount | Fee / month | Worth it from (GVB) | Worth it above (spend / month) |
| -------- | ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 10%      | €1.00       | ~4 trips/month      | €10                            |
| 20%      | €5.50       | ~17 trips/month     | €27.50                         |
| 40%      | €15.00      | ~20 trips/month     | €37.50                         |

The trip counts are GVB's own estimates. The spend column is the exact break-even: what you'd spend on GVB in a month without any discount.

- Works all day, every day. No peak or off-peak rules.
- Covers every GVB tram, bus, metro and night bus.
- Doesn't cover NS trains, or buses run by other companies like Connexxion or EBS.
- You can switch tiers or cancel each month.

## Is it worth it?

Which tier saves you the most depends on your monthly spend:

- **Under about €45 a month:** 10%
- **Over about €47.50 a month:** 40%
- 20% only wins in the small gap between those two.

**Assumptions for the examples below:**

- An example fare of **€2.00 per trip**
- **One return trip (2 trips) per office day**, so 2 office days = 2 return trips a week
- **52 ÷ 12 ≈ 4.33 weeks** in a month
- Commuting only, no weekend trips
- 2026 GVB prices

| Office days / week | Trips / month | Spend / month | Best tier | Saves / month |
| ------------------ | ------------- | ------------- | --------- | ------------- |
| 2                  | ~17           | ~€35          | 10%       | ~€2.50        |
| 3                  | ~26           | ~€52          | 40%       | ~€5.80        |

## Weekends count too

It's not just for commuting. Weekend trips into town, to the market or to a friend's place get the same discount, so they all add to your monthly total.
